Potion Drop

A physics-based puzzle game - drop potions and watch them merge!

How to Play:

  • Click or tap anywhere to drop a potion
  • Potions of the same size will merge when they touch
  • Create larger and larger potions by strategic placement
  • Don't let potions reach the top of the screen!

Game Mechanics:

  • Dropping: Click anywhere to drop the next potion
  • Merging: Same-sized potions combine into larger ones
  • Physics: Potions bounce and roll with realistic physics
  • Scoring: Higher scores for larger potion combinations
  • Game Over: When potions reach the top of the screen
